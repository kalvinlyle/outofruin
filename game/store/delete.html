Delete your Out of Ruin data

Out of Ruin is published by Misaligned. You can ask us to delete the data the app has collected about your device at any time. You do not need an account.

How to request deletion

  1. In the game, open Settings and tap delete my data. This page opens with your device’s identifier filled in. (Or find your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads and paste it below.)
  2. Add your email address, so we can confirm when it is done.
  3. Tap Submit deletion request. Your email app opens with the request written out; send it.
  4. We delete the data within 30 days and confirm by email.

We filled your identifier in for you from the game. Add your email address below.

What is deleted

  • Gameplay analytics events stored under your device identifier (PostHog, and Supercell’s analytics service).
  • Install attribution data stored under your advertising ID (AppsFlyer).
  • Your email address and this request, once we have confirmed the deletion.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records (the order ID, product and date of any gold you bought) are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires, up to 7 years, and are not used for anything else.
  • Your game save lives only on your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes it.
  • Without a request, analytics and attribution data are deleted automatically on our providers’ retention schedule.
