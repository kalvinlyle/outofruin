Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

This page is a pre-launch preview for Out of Ruin, operated by Misaligned ("we", "us"). It helps us gauge interest in upcoming games before release. Questions: support@misaligned.games.

What we collect

  • Anonymous usage events — page views and button taps, with a random session identifier (not tied to your identity).
  • Campaign attribution — the ad source/campaign parameters (UTM) and referrer in the link you arrived from.
  • Email address — only if you choose to submit it to be notified at launch.

Why

To measure which game concepts and ads people respond to, and to notify interested people when a game launches. We do not build advertising profiles from this data.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. Event data is stored with our infrastructure provider (Supabase) solely to produce aggregate interest metrics.

If you install the Android app

Once Out of Ruin is released and you install it from an app store, the app additionally uses:

  • PostHog analytics (EU-hosted) — anonymous product-analytics events, your device’s Android device identifier (ANDROID_ID) as the id those events are stored under, and approximate (country-level) location derived from your IP address. Not tied to your name or email; we do not sell it.
  • AppsFlyer install attribution — your device’s advertising ID (GAID) and basic install/app-open events, to measure which ad campaigns lead to installs (the app declares the com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID permission). AppsFlyer processes this on our behalf; aggregate campaign results are shared with our advertising partners (e.g. AppLovin). We do not use it to build an advertising profile of you. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Data is encrypted in transit. On request we delete analytics and attribution data associated with your device (see below).

In the app: your game and purchases

  • Your game save stays on your device. Progress is stored locally by the app and is not uploaded to us.
  • Purchases of gold in the Android app are made through Google Play. Google processes the payment; we receive the purchase’s order and token from Google to confirm it and deliver the gold, and keep a record of it for accounting. On the website, payments are processed by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.
  • Game updates are downloaded from our own server; that request carries the app and game version and an anonymous install identifier, used only to deliver the right update.
  • Supercell analytics — the same anonymous gameplay events described above may also be sent to an analytics service operated for us by Supercell Oy, keyed to the same device identifier, to evaluate the game. They are not used for advertising.

Your choices

To access or delete any data associated with you (including an email you submitted), email support@misaligned.games. So we can find your data, include your device’s advertising ID (Android Settings → Privacy → Ads — the ID is shown at the bottom of that screen): attribution data is keyed to it and we will delete it via our provider. Gameplay analytics are stored under the Android device identifier (ANDROID_ID) named above rather than your name or email; the same request covers them, and they are deleted automatically per our retention schedule in any case. You can also sever attribution yourself at any time by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in the same Android settings screen; the Android device identifier changes only when the device is factory reset. You can also request deletion with the data deletion form, which the app’s Settings screen opens with your identifier filled in.
