# img

Images for the marketing site and the game's art pipeline.

- Root files (`masthead.jpg`, `logo_oor.*`, badges, mockups) — marketing-site art.
- `heroes/` — hero card art and thumbnails used on the site.
- `henchmen/` — the henchmen card art packed into the game; see its README.
- `gear/` — the equipment card art packed into the game; see its README.
- `items/` — the drop icons packed into the game, two sizes per item; see its README.
- `lootbox/` — the reward chests, closed and open, packed into the game; see its README.
- `map/` — the region terrain paintings behind the map and dungeon screens; see its README.
- `location/` — the dungeon location icons on the dungeon screen's tab bar; see its README.
- `npc/` — quest-giver medallions packed into the game, such as Old Hazel for Night of the Wererabbit.
- `ui/` — loose interface art packed into the main atlas: the gold coin and its six spin frames, hero power icons.
- `_visdev/artstyle-validation/` — samples generated from each `ARTSTYLE.md` block.
- `_trash/` — soft-deleted assets (local only).
