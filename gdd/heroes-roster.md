# Hero Roster — 20 New Heroes

A paper design for twenty new heroes, joining the five in the game today
(Urlith, Sylaei, Zes, Bram and the unnamed Human Cleric).

Every hero does one thing no other hero does. Two heroes may touch the same
resource, but never through the same mechanic.

## The power must be seen

How a power is drawn matters more than its numbers. A player should
understand a hero by watching them work once, without reading anything.
Every hero below therefore has a **How you see it** line, and each one obeys
three rules:

- **It starts at the hero.** A field hero's power leaves their card on the
  location painting. A patron hero appears in person on the one screen where
  their bonus applies.
- **It lands on something already on screen**: a level bar, a loot icon and
  its counter, a hire button, a quest card, the sell screen, the pack
  opening, the welcome-back popup.
- **The change is counted in front of you.** Numbers tick up or down while
  you watch, rather than jumping.

A power that changes something the player cannot see, such as a level that
is not open yet, is redesigned until it passes.

## Two kinds of hero

- **Field heroes** are assigned to a dungeon slot, like the five shipped
  heroes. Their power fires on a cooldown in that dungeon only, so where you
  put them is the decision.
- **Patron heroes** are never assigned. Owning one switches on a permanent
  bonus, the way a researcher does in AdVenture Communist. Each level roughly
  doubles the bonus (×2 at level 1, ×4 at 2, ×8 at 3 and so on). Scope
  follows rarity: a Rare patron boosts one region or one system, an Epic
  patron a whole system, and the Legendary patron boosts the other heroes.

Both kinds level from duplicate cards plus XP, on the existing hero level
curve (levels 1 to 13). Numbers below are at level 1 and level 13, and grow
evenly in between.

All twenty are in the game, locked, and kept off the cards screen. How each
one unlocks is not decided yet.

## Virtues

Each hero has two or three virtues, in order. Virtues come in opposed pairs
(Empathy / Greed, Reason / Wrath, Zeal / Stasis), and a hero never holds both
halves of a pair. The order sets the hero's art colours, as in
[ARTSTYLE.md](../ARTSTYLE.md): the first virtue lights the figure, the second
is the background, the third is a minor highlight.

| Virtue | Stat | Colour |
|---|---|---|
| Wrath | Attack | crimson |
| Greed | Luck | green |
| Zeal | Speed | gold |
| Stasis | Defense | grey-purple |
| Reason | Wisdom | blue |
| Empathy | Healing | pink |

Each hero's lead virtue matches what their power does: Wrath heroes hit,
Greed heroes find, Zeal heroes hurry, Stasis heroes hold and keep, Reason
heroes plan and convert, Empathy heroes lead and look after.

Every hero has a different main colour pair (first and second virtue) from
every other hero, shipped or new, except Sir Aldous, who shares Bram's gold on
blue sky because he trained him.

## Field heroes

### 1. Grisla Hexmire — Bog Hag

- **Who:** an old female hag with a crooked grin and a hat full of bottled
  frogs, from the Forest Ruin bogs.
- **Virtues:** Wrath, Stasis, Greed (crimson figure, grey-purple background,
  green highlight).
- **Power — Hex.** Every 45 s, curses the dungeon for 20 s: every level there
  takes +50% damage from henchmen and every hero power. Level 13:
  +200%.
- **How you see it:** a crimson rune leaps from her card and splits onto
  every level bar, which turns cracked and crimson while the curse lasts,
  with a shrinking ring showing the time left. Every damage number on a
  cursed level pops bigger, in crimson, with a "+50%" tag beside it.
- **Why she is unique:** she deals no damage herself. She multiplies everyone
  else, so she belongs in the dungeon where the strongest damage already is.

### 2. Brakka Ironjaw — Orc Siege-Breaker

- **Who:** a huge female orc with an iron jaw-plate and a battering ram she
  carries like a walking stick.
- **Virtues:** Wrath, Zeal, Greed (crimson figure, gold background, green
  highlight).
- **Power — Breach.** Every 60 s, hits every level in the dungeon for 3% of
  that level's full health. Level 13: 15%.
- **How you see it:** she charges down the level list and her ram slams each
  bar in turn, knocking out a visible chunk exactly 3% of the bar wide, with a
  crack and a "−3%" tag. The chunk is the same width on every bar, which is
  the point: tough levels lose as much as easy ones.
- **Why she is unique:** every other damage power is a flat number that
  shrinks as levels get tougher. Breach scales with the level, so she matters
  as much in Chapter 90 as in Chapter 9.

### 3. Nell Hookline — Human Fisherwoman

- **Who:** a sturdy, weather-beaten female fisher in an oilskin hat, with a
  pipe, a bent rod and endless patience.
- **Virtues:** Greed, Reason, Stasis (green figure, blue background,
  grey-purple highlight).
- **Power — Gone Fishing.** Every 60 s she reels in one catch from her own
  catch table:

  | Chance | Catch | Where it goes |
  |---|---|---|
  | 60% | Junk: an old boot, a dented helmet, driftwood, a tin cup | Sell screen, for gold |
  | 25% | A net of this dungeon's drops | Straight into that loot |
  | 10% | A message in a bottle | XP |
  | 4% | A fat fish | Followers |
  | 1% | A sealed chest: a gear card or a hero card | Card collection |

  Level 13: three catches a cast, and the odds of everything but junk double.
- **How you see it:** her line flicks out of her card onto the water in the
  location painting, the bobber bobs, then a splash and the catch flies up
  over her card, wiggles, and travels to where it goes: junk drops into a
  sack icon that fills up for the sell screen, drops fly into their loot
  counter, the bottle into the XP button, the fish into the followers
  counter. The rare chest stops in the middle of the screen and opens.
- **Why she is unique:** she is a small loot box on a timer. Most catches are
  junk, so she never replaces any other hero, but every minute is a
  surprise and now and then it is a big one.

### 4. Old Mossback — Troll Beastmaster

- **Who:** a hunched, mossy male troll with a soft spot for animals, a badger
  on his shoulder and a wolf at his knee.
- **Virtues:** Wrath, Empathy, Zeal (crimson figure, pink background, gold
  highlight).
- **Power — Pounce.** Every 40 s, his beasts finish off every level in the
  dungeon that is below 10% health. Level 13: below 35%.
- **How you see it:** while he is assigned, every level bar in his dungeon
  carries a small claw-mark tick at 10%, so you can see which levels are in
  reach. When a bar drops under the tick it gets a paw-print badge. When he
  fires, his wolf leaps from his card onto each badged bar and the bar
  empties.
- **Why he is unique:** the only execute. He does nothing to fresh levels and
  everything to wounded ones, so he pairs with heroes who do the wounding.

### 5. Fizzwick Cogsworth — Gnome Tinkerer

- **Who:** a tiny male gnome with goggles, a wrench and a clanking clockwork
  turret on a tripod.
- **Virtues:** Reason, Stasis, Greed (blue figure, grey-purple background,
  green highlight).
- **Power — Clockwork Turret.** Sets up a turret on the deepest open level
  that deals steady damage every second for as long as Fizzwick stays
  assigned, instead of firing in bursts. 1 damage/s, level 13: 40/s.
- **How you see it:** a little brass turret sits at the end of that level's
  bar, ticking and firing a small bolt every second. The bar drains in a
  smooth, steady slide, with a damage-per-second label on the turret.
- **Why he is unique:** the only constant damage. Every other power waits on
  a cooldown; the turret never stops.

### 6. Madame Ottoline — Elf Alchemist

- **Who:** a tall, elegant female elf with a smoking cauldron belt and one
  eyebrow permanently raised.
- **Virtues:** Reason, Greed, Zeal (blue figure, green background, gold
  highlight).
- **Power — Transmute.** Every 90 s, turns 10% of the dungeon's most common
  drop into its rarest drop, at a rate of 10 to 1. Level 13: 30% at 4 to 1.
- **How you see it:** a stream of the common item (say turnips) flies off its
  loot icon and into her card, and that counter ticks down as they go. Her
  cauldron bubbles and puffs, a "10 → 1" tag floats up, and the rare item
  flies out of her card into its own loot icon, whose counter ticks up with a
  sparkle. Both counters move while you watch, so the trade is obvious.
- **Why she is unique:** the only hero who converts one resource into
  another. She fixes the bottleneck instead of adding more of everything.

### 7. Captain Hollis — Human Recruiting Officer

- **Who:** a broad, bushy-moustached male captain with a drum, a ledger and a
  voice that carries three valleys.
- **Virtues:** Empathy, Zeal, Reason (pink figure, gold background, blue
  highlight).
- **Power — Muster.** Every 5 min, hires one henchman for free on a random
  open level of the dungeon. Level 13: seven at a time.
- **How you see it:** a drum roll, then a little henchman marches out of his
  card and down to one level row, where the henchman count ticks up by one
  and the hire button flashes a "FREE" stamp.
- **Why he is unique:** the only hero who adds henchmen. Everyone else makes
  levels or loot move faster; he grows the crew.

### 8. Lady Vesper — Ghost of the Lantern

- **Who:** a calm female ghost in an old-fashioned gown, holding a lantern
  that keeps the memory of everything it has lit.
- **Virtues:** Stasis, Empathy, Reason (grey-purple figure, pink background,
  blue highlight).
- **Power — Echo.** Every 60 s, repeats the last power any other hero fired
  in her dungeon, at 40% strength. Level 13: 100%.
- **How you see it:** her power icon always shows a small portrait of the
  hero she will echo next, so you know in advance. When she fires, a
  translucent grey-purple ghost of that hero rises from her card and plays
  their power's effect again, faded.
- **Why she is unique:** she has no effect of her own. She is a second copy
  of whichever hero you put beside her, so she rewards building a dungeon
  around one star.

### 9. Thumper — Wererabbit Hopper (event hero)

- **Who:** a stocky, cheerful male wererabbit with huge feet and dirt on his
  nose, from Night of the Wererabbit.
- **Virtues:** Zeal, Greed, Wrath (gold figure, green background, crimson
  highlight).
- **Power — Hopscotch.** Every 50 s, hops down the dungeon's open levels from
  top to bottom, hitting each one. The first hop deals 2 damage and every hop
  doubles it, so the deepest, toughest level takes the biggest hit. Level 13:
  starts at 40.
- **How you see it:** a rabbit token bounces from his card onto the top level
  bar, then hops down row by row. Each landing shakes the bar, kicks up a
  bigger dust puff and shows a bigger number (2, 4, 8, 16…).
- **Why he is unique:** the only power that grows as it travels. Chain
  Lightning hits every level the same; Hopscotch saves its biggest hit for
  the end of the list, so he gets stronger as the dungeon opens up.

### 10. Wren Lightfoot — Human Bard

- **Who:** a young female bard with a lute strapped across her back, mid-step
  and mid-song.
- **Virtues:** Zeal, Empathy, Reason (gold figure, pink background, blue
  highlight).
- **Power — Ballad of Deeds.** Every 3 min she sings about one of your active
  chapter quests that asks for this dungeon's drops, and adds 5% of that
  quest's goal to it. Level 13: 20%. If no active quest asks for this
  dungeon's drops, she has nothing to sing about and her power icon shows a
  resting lute.
- **How you see it:** music notes stream out of her card and up to that
  quest's card in the header. Its progress bar jumps forward and the counter
  ticks up (say 120/500 → 145/500), and a note sticks to the card for a
  moment.
- **Why she is unique:** the only hero who works on quests rather than on
  levels or loot, so you send her wherever a quest is waiting.

## Patron heroes

Patrons are never on the dungeon screen. Each appears in person on the one
screen where their bonus applies, every time it applies.

### 11. Barnaby Hearth — Halfling Innkeeper

- **Who:** a round, beaming male halfling innkeeper with a towel over one
  shoulder and a tray of mugs.
- **Virtues:** Empathy, Stasis, Reason (pink figure, grey-purple background,
  blue highlight).
- **Bonus — Warm Hearth.** Followers earned while you are away are ×2.
  Level 13: ×64.
- **How you see it:** on the welcome-back popup, the base number counts up
  first ("You earned 1,990 followers"), then Barnaby leans in from the side,
  pours a mug, and the number counts up again under a "×2 Warm Hearth" line.
- **Why he is unique:** the only patron who pays while you are away.

### 12. Henrietta Plough — Human Farm Matriarch

- **Who:** a stout, sunburned female farmer with a pitchfork and a rooster
  that follows her everywhere.
- **Virtues:** Reason, Zeal, Empathy (blue figure, gold background, pink
  highlight).
- **Bonus — Early Riser.** Every henchman in the Farm region works ×2 as fast.
  Level 13: ×64.
- **How you see it:** in Farm dungeons her rooster perches on top of the
  level list and crows when you arrive. Every henchman card there wears a
  small rooster badge with "×2", and their progress bars run with a gold
  shimmer.
- **Why she is unique:** the only regional patron. She is the template for a
  later series, one patron per region (Desert, Forest, Mountain, Mine), each
  a different hero.

### 13. Ledger Goldwhistle — Goblin Moneylender

- **Who:** a skinny male goblin in a waistcoat and a monocle, counting coins
  on his long fingers.
- **Virtues:** Greed, Wrath, Stasis (green figure, crimson background,
  grey-purple highlight).
- **Bonus — Friends' Rates.** Henchmen cost 10% less to hire. Level 13: 60%
  less.
- **How you see it:** on every hire button, the full cost is shown struck
  through with the lower cost beside it and a tiny monocle stamp. On a hire,
  Ledger's hand flicks the saved coins back into your counters.
- **Why he is unique:** the only discount. Every other bonus gives more;
  Ledger makes things cost less.

### 14. Seraphine Vale — Human Fortune Teller

- **Who:** a dramatic female fortune teller with a crystal ball, a shawl of
  coins and very knowing eyes.
- **Virtues:** Greed, Zeal, Reason (green figure, gold background, blue
  highlight).
- **Bonus — Read the Cards.** +5% loot crit chance on every level in every
  dungeon, on top of what gear gives that level. A loot crit is a drop that
  pays bonus loot on top, and a crit of hers pays at least double. It applies
  to every drop on those levels, whoever earns it: henchmen and hero powers.
  Level 13: +30%.
- **How you see it:** whenever a crit lands that she caused, her crystal ball
  pops up over the loot icon, flashes green, and the bonus counts in under a
  "Lucky!" tag.
- **Why she is unique:** the only source of loot crit chance outside gear,
  and the only one that covers every level at once.

### 15. Tomas Quill — Dwarf Sage

- **Who:** an old male dwarf scholar, beard tucked into his belt, peering
  over a stack of books taller than him.
- **Virtues:** Empathy, Reason, Stasis (pink figure, blue background,
  grey-purple highlight).
- **Bonus — Lesson Plans.** +20% XP from every source. Level 13: +300%.
- **How you see it:** whenever XP is earned, Tomas's quill writes a second
  line under the amount, "+20% Lesson Plans", and the XP counter ticks up
  twice.
- **Why he is unique:** the only patron that speeds up levelling heroes
  themselves, since hero levels cost XP.

### 16. Gorvo the Fence — Ogre Pawnbroker

- **Who:** a huge, gentle-looking male ogre behind a tiny counter, with a
  loupe screwed into one eye.
- **Virtues:** Wrath, Greed, Zeal (crimson figure, green background, gold
  highlight).
- **Bonus — No Questions Asked.** Items sell for ×2 gold. Level 13: ×20.
- **How you see it:** Gorvo stands behind the counter at the top of the sell
  screen. When it opens, he peers at each item through his loupe and stamps
  it: the normal price shows first, then his stamp lands and the price
  counts up to his price. When you sell, he slaps the coins down on the
  counter.
- **Why he is unique:** the only patron for the sell screen, which also makes
  Nell's junk worth fishing for.

### 17. Mother Mabb — Gnome Card Collector

- **Who:** a tiny, fierce old female gnome buried in a cloak sewn from
  playing cards.
- **Virtues:** Empathy, Wrath, Stasis (pink figure, crimson background,
  grey-purple highlight).
- **Bonus — Lucky Draw.** Every card pack holds one extra card. Level 13:
  three extra, and every pack holds at least one Epic.
- **How you see it:** when a pack opens and its cards are dealt, Mabb pops up
  at the edge of the screen and flicks her extra cards onto the pile, each
  with her pink-backed card and a "Mabb's pick" ribbon, so you can tell
  which cards were hers.
- **Why she is unique:** the only patron for card packs, so she speeds up
  every collection at once: gear, henchmen and heroes.

### 18. Tock — Clockwork Golem

- **Who:** a squat bronze golem with a clock face for a chest and a big
  wind-up key in its back. It has no gender.
- **Virtues:** Stasis, Wrath, Empathy (grey-purple figure, crimson
  background, pink highlight).
- **Bonus — Wound Spring.** Every time warp skips 10% more time. Level 13:
  twice as much.
- **How you see it:** on the time warp card, the warp you bought shows first,
  then Tock steps in, winds its key, and the card counts up to the stretched
  warp with a "Wound Spring" line.
- **Why it is unique:** the only patron that changes time itself. Barnaby
  raises what time away pays; Tock stretches the time warps you buy.

### 19. Grubb — Kobold Notice Runner

- **Who:** a small, eager male kobold with a hammer, a mouthful of nails and
  a satchel of quest notices.
- **Virtues:** Stasis, Greed, Reason (grey-purple figure, green background,
  blue highlight).
- **Bonus — Notice Runner.** One extra chapter quest runs at the same time.
  Level 7: two extra. Level 13: three extra.
- **How you see it:** in the header, Grubb scampers in and nails an extra
  quest card into the row beside the others, wearing a small Grubb tag. When
  a quest finishes, he runs in and nails up the next.
- **Why he is unique:** the only patron for chapter quests. More quests at
  once means less waiting on any single one, so every chapter moves faster.

### 20. Sir Aldous Brightwater — Retired Knight

- **Who:** a white-moustached, barrel-chested old male knight in polished
  armour that no longer quite fits, who trained Bram.
- **Virtues:** Zeal, Reason (gold figure, blue-sky background, like Bram).
- **Bonus — Old Master.** Every other hero's power counts as one level
  higher. Level 13: five levels higher.
- **How you see it:** every other hero's card carries Aldous's small gold
  crest beside their level, showing "+1". When any hero's power fires, a gold
  glint runs round that crest.
- **Why he is unique:** the only hero who improves other heroes. He is the
  Legendary chase card, the way a Supreme researcher is in AdVenture
  Communist.
