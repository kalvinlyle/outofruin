# AdVenture Capitalist — browser-born idle tycoon and the prestige-reset template

## Snapshot

- **Studio:** Hyper Hippo Productions, built in Unity. iOS version published by Kongregate. Single-player. Source: [Wikipedia: AdVenture Capitalist](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdVenture_Capitalist).
- **Platforms:** Browser (May 30, 2014), Android (December 2014), iOS (February 18, 2015), Windows and OS X (March 30, 2015), Linux (July 24, 2015), PlayStation 4 (August 16, 2016, North America). Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdVenture_Capitalist).
- **Release and status:** Mature live title with many releases over a decade. Expansions added Moon and Mars investments. A separate sequel-style spin-off, AdVenture Ages, shares the lineage.
- **Monetisation:** Free-to-play. Gold is bought with real money, but a small amount can be earned for free. Gold buys Angel Investors without resetting progress. MegaBucks come from limited-time events, watching ads and first-time Earth milestones, and can also be bought with a price that rises after each purchase. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdVenture_Capitalist).
- **Audience scale (non-audited):** Not verified in this pass. The spin-off AdVenture Communist reported over five million installs as of December 2018 on the same Wikipedia page, which is a sibling-title figure and not the parent game's audience.
- **Review sentiment:** Mixed in critical press. Pocket Gamer gave 3/10, 148Apps 3.5/5, and Push Square 6.3/10. Critics said progress was poorly rewarded, achievements were dull, and in-app purchases were too expensive. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdVenture_Capitalist). Store ratings reported in search results include a PlayStation Store average of 3.67 stars from about 39K ratings, not audited.
- **Player promise:** Build a business empire from a lemonade stand, reset for Angel Investors, and watch the next run move faster.

## Key innovations / what it does well

- **Prestige reset as the core loop.** Resetting progress grants Angel Investors, which boost all product revenue in the next timeline. The reset is the central decision, and the game makes its payoff visible before you commit. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdVenture_Capitalist). This became the template for the idle genre, including [Cookie Clicker's Heavenly Chips](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cookie_Clicker) and Egg, Inc.'s soul eggs.
- **Gold buys Angel Investors without resetting.** A premium path that accelerates prestige rather than replacing it, which keeps the free loop intact. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdVenture_Capitalist).
- **Time-limited events for a second currency.** MegaBucks from events and milestones give players a reason to return on a schedule. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdVenture_Capitalist).
- **Cross-platform reach from a browser start.** The game started as a browser title in 2014 and reached mobile, desktop and console. That gave it a long tail on every device a casual player already owned.
- **Expansion of the world map.** Moon and Mars investments extended a finished loop with new content rather than a new game. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdVenture_Capitalist).

## Player frictions & complaints

- **Angel-multiplier changes.** A Steam community thread shows players reacting to a 2016 patch that changed the angel bonus, with one noting per-angel bonuses went from 30% to 77.7%. Source: [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/346900/discussions/0/359543951706118393/?l=romanian). This shows how sensitive players are to changes in prestige math.
- **Poor progress rewards.** Critics said progress felt poorly rewarded and the loop was a "pointless waste of time." Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdVenture_Capitalist).
- **Expensive IAP and dull achievements.** Critics called in-app purchases too expensive and achievements dull. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdVenture_Capitalist).
- **Illusion of progress.** Critics of [Cookie Clicker](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cookie_Clicker) made the same charge of progress without substance, and the same idea applies to idle loops generally. AdVenture Capitalist-specific criticism of this kind was not found in this pass.
- **Spin-off comparisons.** Reviews of AdVenture Communist said it was too similar to its predecessor and heavy on microtransactions, which shows the audience's expectations were shaped by the parent. Source: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AdVenture_Capitalist).

## Lessons for Out of Ruin

- **Make the prestige payoff visible before the reset.** AdVenture Capitalist's Investor tab shows how many Angel Investors a reset will earn. Out of Ruin's chapter ranking and unlock list should preview what the next rank grants before a player commits.
- **Keep premium currency on the same loop, not beside it.** Gold buying Angel Investors without resetting is a model that accelerates the core path rather than paywalling it. Gold-for-card-packs in Out of Ruin should follow that shape.
- **Tune multipliers as a public contract.** The 2016 angel change drew a visible player reaction. Out of Ruin's crit and gear-card boosts should be documented in the game so changes are understood, not discovered.
- **Expect spin-off comparisons.** AdVenture Communist was judged against its parent. Any Out of Ruin expansion should be framed as its own loop, not a reskin of the existing one.
- **Treat achievements as a real reward, not a checklist.** Reviews call AdVenture Capitalist's achievements dull. Out of Ruin's milestone progression should give each milestone a visible change in the world.
