Where Out of Ruin lands

The games with deep hero and gear collections (AFK Arena, Idle Heroes, Tap Titans 2) sell power, and the games players call fair (Egg, Inc., Idle Slayer, Cookie Clicker) have shallow rosters. Out of Ruin already has heroes, henchmen and gear cards; committing to published odds, pity and everything-earnable progression puts it alone in the top-right.
