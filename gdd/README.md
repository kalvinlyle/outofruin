# gdd/

Game design documentation for Out of Ruin: design analysis, competitor research and player research.
Code, build notes and recovered source do not belong here.

- [chapter-0.md](./chapter-0.md) — the first-time experience: a guided opening on the Farmyard, three quests, then Chapter 1.
- [direction.md](./direction.md) — the design commitments: native app, no ads, back-to-back events winnable free-to-play, 101 chapters, pay-to-progress shop.
- [heroes-roster.md](./heroes-roster.md) — twenty new heroes on paper: field heroes with assigned powers and patron heroes with passive bonuses, each with virtues, role, power and how the power is shown on screen.
- [live-events.md](./live-events.md) — how live events work, and the events in the rotation: Night of the Wererabbit and the three threat events.
- [competitor-analysis.md](./competitor-analysis.md) — landscape synthesis: rings, release chronology, wedges and the strengths/weaknesses matrix.
- [competitor-research/](./competitor-research/) — one teardown per competitor (full or light tier), with sourced claims.
- [player-research/](./player-research/) — staged audience research, ending in `win-strategy.md`.
- [competitor-axis.html](./competitor-axis.html) — interactive positioning plot (collection depth vs fairness to free players); data in `competitor-axis.data.js`, renderer in `competitor-axis.render.js`.
