# Out of Ruin — Design Direction

The commitments that shape the next version of Out of Ruin.
The competitor research that informs them is in [competitor-analysis.md](./competitor-analysis.md).

## Platform

- Out of Ruin ships as a **native iOS and Android app** with local saves.
- The web build is for development and testing.

## No ads, ever

- No rewarded ads, no interstitials, no banners, no ad-removal purchase.
- Offline income and every earned reward are collected free.

## Events, back to back

- Events run back to back, and can overlap, on a repeating rotation.
- New events enter the rotation as fast as they can be made.
- Every event is guaranteed winnable free-to-play, through effort alone: the final reward tier never depends on good luck or on spending.
- The world map is how players move between the main game and events: each live event appears as a location on it.

## Content scale

- 90 more chapters, for 101 in total.
- More heroes, henchmen and characters to fill them.
- More locations beyond the current five regions.

## Shop: pay to progress, not pay to win

- The shop sells permanent upgrades that speed progress.
- Everything a purchase gives can also be reached by playing.
