# Art Style

One visual identity applied to everything. No variations, no
exceptions, no overrides.

## Prompt

Prepend to every generation prompt:

> Chunky hand-sculpted forms with exaggerated proportions, heavily
> rounded bevels, and bold silhouettes — built from a few big simple
> volumes, no thin parts, no small details, no filigree. Rendered
> with expressive painterly brushwork and dramatic mood-driven
> lighting. Saturated emotionally-coded palettes bathe each subject
> in a dominant hue. Warm glow in lit areas, cool complementary
> shadows. Visible painted strokes over smooth sculpted geometry.
> Focal point gets moderate detail, everything else is broad flat
> color masses. Matte to satin base with tinted specular and
> saturated rim glow. No outlines, no thin lines, no fine textures,
> no intricate patterns. Each subject is one clear chunky readable
> silhouette.

## Core Identity

- **Chunky simplified shapes first.** Everything is built from a few
  big, bold, rounded volumes. No thin details, no small parts, no
  filigree. If a shape can be simplified into fewer pieces, it must
  be. Silhouette readability at arm's length is the primary test.
- **Dramatic mood-driven lighting.** Saturated colored key lights
  that emotionally code every scene. Rim lighting glows with
  saturated color for silhouette separation.
- **No outlines.** Form boundaries emerge from painted value contrast
  and saturated rim glow. Never ink strokes, dark-outline passes, or
  drawn edges.
- **Painterly over sculpted.** Visible brushwork and painted strokes
  ride on top of smooth rounded 3D geometry. Matte to satin base,
  never glossy or mirror-like.
- **Saturated emotional palettes.** Entire subjects bathed in
  dominant hues. Warm glow in lit areas, cool complementary plunge
  in shadows. Theatrical and expressive, never neutral.
- **Ruthless detail hierarchy.** The focal point gets moderate
  painterly attention. Everything else reduces to big flat color
  masses with almost no internal detail. When in doubt, remove
  detail.
- **Tactile sculptural presence.** Everything reads as chunky,
  hand-sculpted, and weighty. Rounded bevels, exaggerated
  proportions. Shape and mass carry character before detail does.
  Fewer parts, bigger parts.

## 7-Aspect Breakdown

### 1. Shading

Dramatic mood-driven lighting with saturated colored key lights.
Soft shadow transitions on rounded forms, plunging deep into cool
complementary tones. Saturated rim glow for silhouette separation.
Ambient occlusion grounds forms with natural contact shadows.
Highlights are broad and tinted — never neutral white. Every
lighting setup is emotionally coded, not neutrally descriptive.

### 2. Edges

No outlines, no ink strokes. Boundaries emerge from painted value
contrast and saturated rim glow. Visible brushwork rides on top of
smooth sculpted geometry — painted edge accents where light meets
shadow, lost-and-found edges where forms dissolve into mood
lighting. Underlying forms stay rounded and beveled; rendering on
top carries expressive hand-painted energy.

### 3. Surface Detail

Minimal detail, maximum shape. Broad folds, big chunky shapes,
simple panels — never thin lines, small rivets, fine traces, or
intricate patterns. The focal point gets moderate painted attention;
everything else reduces to big flat color masses with almost no
internal detail. Always err on the side of less. If a detail doesn't
change the silhouette, remove it.

### 4. Color

Saturated, emotionally coded palettes that bathe the entire subject
in a dominant hue. Warm glow in lit areas, cool complementary plunge
in shadows. Colors transition through painted blending with visible
brush energy, not smooth digital gradients. Theatrical and
expressive — never neutral, never naturalistic. Saturated accents
against desaturated bases.

### 5. Form Language

Chunky, organically sculpted forms with exaggerated proportions.
Built from a few big rounded volumes — no small parts, no thin
elements, no fiddly geometry. Rounded, heavily beveled surfaces with
real sculptural weight, like soft clay pressurized from inside. No
angular faceting — stylization comes from proportion and mass, not
geometric planes. Weighty, tactile, toylike. There is no
"restrained" register.

### 6. Material Rendering

Painterly and expressive over a matte-to-satin base. Visible
painted strokes and tonal variation across every surface. Specular
hits are saturated and tinted by the scene's mood light, never
neutral. Broad painted folds with warm-cool color shifts. Each
material is distinguishable but unified by the same expressive
painted-over-3D treatment. Translucent edges catch saturated
backlight glow.

### 7. Overall Feel

Chunky hand-sculpted forms rendered with expressive painterly
brushwork and theatrical cinematic lighting. Toylike and tactile —
exaggerated proportions, rounded bevels, bold silhouettes built from
few simple shapes. Emotionally charged — saturated mood color,
visible brush energy, dramatic light-to-shadow contrast. Looks
hand-sculpted then painted over with dramatic stage lighting. Never
photorealistic, never flat vector cartoon, never refined or
intricate.
